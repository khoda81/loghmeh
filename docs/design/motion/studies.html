Motion exploration · three concepts

Three ways to wait for the dining hall.

Motion concepts for the extension’s dining-connection card, drawn live with Canvas and the Web Animations API from local code only. Every state here is simulated. Nothing on this page contacts the University of Tehran dining system, reads a session, or tests a login — the buttons only change the drawing, and Connected appears only when you select it.

برنامهٔ هفته

  1. Checking

    Loops without a destination. No percentages, nothing that fills up.

  2. Open dining

    Comes to rest and points at the one thing the person needs to do.

  3. Connected

    Resolves once, then holds. Never reached automatically.

  4. Cannot reach dining

    Settles into a still, readable picture. No endless alarm.

01

Saffron Orbit مدار زعفران

Rice, herb and saffron-gold grains keep time around the bowl. They move in one-seventh-turn surges — a week per orbit — climb into an arched doorway when they need you, and only fall into the dish when Connected is chosen.

Choreography

Checking
1.7 s beat. Each ring surges ⅐ or ⅑ of a turn (cubic in-out over 60% of the beat), then rests. Rings are offset 220 ms and grains ripple with 0–120 ms micro-delays; orbits breathe in 4.5% per surge. Saffron leaves a trail only while it moves.
Open
Grains climb into a pointed arch (طاق) over 1.0 s, staggered 0–550 ms along the path; a sage niche fills behind the bowl. One saffron thread hangs in the keystone gap and nods twice every 3.2 s. Everything else is still.
Connected
Spiral pour, 850 ms per grain, staggered bottom-up across 1.0 s so the mound builds from its base. Saffron lands from 1.2 s, the bowl gives once (3%), a copper ring seals at 1.85 s, steam drifts.
Cannot reach
Grains accelerate downward and settle without bouncing (1.15 s, 0–300 ms random delays) and lie on the table; the outer orbit is drawn broken. Nothing moves after ~1.5 s.

Score first 5 s of the selected state

BuildCanvas 2D. 86 seeded grains (44 in the card), one path each; only the 8 saffron threads sample 9 past poses for trails. Deterministic, so every replay is identical.

TradeoffsMost cinematic and food-forward; reads as “grains” even at 200 px. Pixel-drawn, so meaning lives in the caption. rAF stops when paused, off-screen or reduced.

02

Loom of Letters دار واژه

A small loom weaves from right to left. While checking it rehearses a kilim border and always reels it back — weaving, never filling. When Connected is chosen it weaves the word لقمه from a glyph mask, row by row.

Choreography

Checking
4.5 s cycle: four boustrophedon passes of 850 ms (sine in-out), the first right-to-left. Stitches pop with a 220 ms back-ease; each warp twangs as the shuttle crosses. 300 ms hold, then the band reels back into the shuttle — so it can never be read as progress.
Open
Threads are set and still. A dotted ghost row draws in once, right to left; the shuttle waits outside the edge with its yarn pointing in and knocks twice every 3.4 s, lighting the first stitch.
Connected
Fifteen rows, bottom to top, ~127 ms each (~1.9 s), copper selvedge rows framing the word. The shuttle parks on the top beam; one sheen crosses the cloth (900 ms), then it holds.
Cannot reach
Stitches drop away, warps go slack with a damped wobble (~1.2 s), one copper thread is visibly snapped, and the shuttle bounces onto the lower beam and rests.

Score first 5 s of the selected state

BuildCanvas 2D, ≤450 cells of two pills each. The word is rasterised once from Estedad 900 into a 30×15 mask (24×12 in the card — coarser cells stay legible at 200 px), re-sampled when the font finishes loading.

TradeoffsMost brand-forward and most Persian: reading direction is part of the motion. Depends on the local font being ready; the cell grid limits which words could ever be woven.

03

Week to Plate از هفته تا بشقاب

Seven day cards, Saturday to Friday, behave like a planner you can hold: they riffle while checking, fan out and wait for you, and fold around a served plate when connected. Friday is dashed — the hall is usually closed.

Choreography

Checking
3.0 s loop. Cards lift 18 px and flip a full turn right-to-left, 210 ms apart (~480 ms each), then ~1.2 s of rest, so the wave reads as a breath rather than a spinner.
Open
Cards fan 9° apart from the centre outwards (760 ms, expo-out) and hold. Wednesday rises under a copper cue; the two nudge together twice every 3.4 s.
Connected
Cards fly to radial seats (~670 ms, 50 ms stagger), the plate inflates with overshoot from 420 ms, cards tuck onto the rim at ~1.0–1.75 s, then rice (1.3 s), saffron (1.56 s) and herbs land; steam loops softly.
Cannot reach
Cards drop into a set-aside stack — Friday first, Saturday on top — with a 60 ms stagger and a land-and-squash, beside an empty dashed plate. Still after ~1.1 s.

Score first 5 s of the selected state

Build~20 elements, transforms and opacity only. Each state reads the live computed transform and animates from it, so interrupting is seamless. Pause = pause every Animation in the panel.

TradeoffsCrispest text, cheapest to run, easiest to hand to production CSS. Less organic than particles; 3D flips need preserve-3d and flatten if an ancestor drops below opacity 1.

Boundaries of this study

  • No network requests, no university endpoints, no cookies or sessions read. State is whatever button you pressed.
  • Connected is never entered automatically; every panel starts in Checking and stays there until you choose otherwise.
  • Pause cancels the canvas rAF loops (time stops advancing) and pauses every Web Animation in the panel, including loops.
  • Reduced motion (system setting, or the toggle above) renders one composed still per state — dashed orbits, a half-woven band, a mid-flip wave — with no loops at all.
  • Assets: local ./Estedad-VF.woff2 and optional ./icon.svg. No CDN, no remote images. Canvases also stop drawing when scrolled off-screen.

Reading the score

  • Solid bars are one-shot beats; hatched copper bars repeat; dotted lines mean the picture holds still.
  • Times are measured from the moment the state is selected (or replayed), on a 0–5 s ruler.
