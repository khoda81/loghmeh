# Saffron Orbit integration

The connection card now adapts the Saffron Orbit study from
`docs/design/motion/studies.html`. The other concepts remain in that gallery.
The illustration is decorative: `ConnectionController` still owns the session
result, and the motion callback only dismisses a confirmed connection notice.

| Session phase | Illustration |
| --- | --- |
| Checking | Indeterminate grain orbits; no progress or automatic success |
| Waiting, redirect, login, permission | Grains form an arch, then hold |
| Network, timeout, server, unexpected error | Rice spills without bouncing, then holds |
| Connected | Rice pours into the bowl; confirmation dismisses after the visible animation |
| Read-only | Served bowl with the limited-access explanation retained |

The planner opens immediately after a successful connection, alongside the
compact confirmation. Pause freezes its playback and dismissal. A result
received while paused renders its composed still; Resume or the dismiss
control closes a completed confirmation. Reduced motion renders composed
stills with no frame loop and gives the success still 1.8 seconds of visible
time. Waiting and failure entrances stop after settling. Visibility changes
suspend frames and the reduced-motion timer; unmount disconnects observers,
listeners, frames and timers. Canvas pixel density is capped at 2.
